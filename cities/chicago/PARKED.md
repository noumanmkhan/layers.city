# Chicago: parked ideas

What Toronto has that the Chicago map doesn't yet, and why. Each entry says what would unpark it.

## Elections

- **Election view for the City Council (February 23, 2027).** Toronto's ward election view (one neutral colour,
  ward labels with the incumbent and candidate count, candidates under the alderperson) fits Chicago's 50
  nonpartisan wards. Unpark once the Board of Elections publishes the certified candidate list for the municipal
  election (nominating petitions are filed in late November 2026): a candidate fetch plus `election` on the wards
  layer and `race` on the Alderperson office. The mayoral race would be a citywide row, as Toronto's is.
- **November 3 results.** The ballot rows stop after election day; results for the legislative and congressional
  races (Cook County Clerk and the Board of Elections) aren't fetched. The school board rows already follow the count.
