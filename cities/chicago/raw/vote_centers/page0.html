Early Voting/Vote Centers

Any voter in Chicago can vote at any Early Voting/Vote Center site, no matter where they live in the city. Voters can choose whatever voting site is most convenient for them, including on Election Day. All locations are ADA-compliant and fully accessible to voters with disabilities. 

Same Day Registration is available at all Early Voting/Vote Center sites, including 51 sites on Election Day. You will need two forms of ID, one with your current address - click here for acceptable forms of ID. Early voters may choose to vote in one of 12 languages: English, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Polish, Korean, Tagalog, Gujarati, Urdu, Arabic, Ukrainian, and Russian.

College/University Early Voting Program

The College/University Early Voting Program establishes temporary additional Early Voting locations for a three-day period on both private and public college and university campuses in Chicago. By bringing the polls directly to campuses, this program aims to make the voting experience more convenient and accessible for students and their surrounding communities. These University Early Voting sites will be open Wednesday, October 28 through Friday, October 30, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.:

  • Chicago State University, 9501 S. King Drive
  • Northeastern Illinois University, 5500 N. St. Louis Avenue
  • University of Chicago - Reynolds Club, 5706 S. University Avenue

Early Voting Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I change my Early Voting ballot after it's been cast?
A: Any ballots that voters cast in Early Voting are final. After casting ballots in Early Voting, voters may not return to amend or change a ballot for any reason. It is a felony to vote more than once -- or to attempt to vote more than once -- in the same election.

Q: Do I need to present my identification for Early Voting?
It's a good idea to carry your government-issued photo identification to Early Voting polls. While identification is not required, it is helpful to bring it in case there is a question about the voter's registration, address, signature, or if there are two voters at the same address with the same name or similar names.
