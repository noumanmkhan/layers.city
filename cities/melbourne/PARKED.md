# Melbourne: not in the first build

The first build (November 12, 2026, unlisted) has the shape and the base layers. What Toronto has that Melbourne
doesn't yet, and what would bring it in. Sources are in the project's claude/australia-scouting.md.

## Next, data in hand or known
- **Where to vote** for the Victorian state election (Saturday November 28, 2026; early voting from November 18). The
  user's call (November 12): build it when the VEC publishes the voting centres as data. The VEC's locator is at
  maps.vec.vic.gov.au; no downloadable list was confirmed.
- **Representatives**: state districts (VEC; 2022 boundaries stand for 2026) and federal divisions (AEC), with MPs. The
  City of Melbourne elects its council at large (no wards).
- **Bus routes** and the Routes page: in the same GTFS (folder 4, metropolitan buses).
- **School zones**: Victorian Government School Zones (DataVic, CC BY 4.0, annual; 2027 zones published).
- **Beach water quality**: EPA Victoria Beach Report (Port Phillip, summer). No open feed found yet.
- **City of Melbourne open data** (Opendatasoft, mostly CC BY 4.0): pedestrian counters, parking bay sensors,
  customer service requests (a 311 fun map), CLUE, trees, drinking fountains, City Activities and Planned Works.
- Hospitals, universities, skyscrapers (150 m suggested), places of worship, venues, events, bike lanes (Transport
  Victoria's Bicycle Infrastructure Network), transit under construction (Suburban Rail Loop East).

## Not planned
- **Getting to work** lens: the 2021 Census counted during lockdown. Revisit with the 2026 Census (journey to work in
  the second release, October 2027).
- **Bike share**: Melbourne has no docked bike share (Melbourne Bike Share closed in 2019).
