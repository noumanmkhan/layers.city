Koorie Krismas

  • Free
  • Family and kids

Koorie Klaus is back to spread festive cheer at the Koorie Heritage Trust’s (KHT) much-loved annual celebration, Koorie Krismas a free, family-friendly event where culture, creativity and joy come together.

On the main stage at Fed Square, First Peoples talent will shine with captivating dance, music, DJ sets and storytelling.

A curated market will feature an incredible lineup of First Peoples artists and makers for all your Krismas shopping needs.

Keep little ones busy with a range of free creative activities throughout the day.

And don’t miss the highlight of the day – a magical appearance from Koorie Klaus himself. One of his cheerful elves will be on hand with a Polaroid camera for family photos to capture the festive fun.
