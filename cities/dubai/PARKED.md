# Dubai: not in the first build

The first build (November 17, 2026, unlisted). What Toronto has that Dubai doesn't yet, and what would bring it in.
Sources and reasons: `scouting/dubai/FINDINGS.md`.

## Waiting on an approval
- **Dubai Municipality's 224 communities and 9 sectors** (Data Dubai 461494, 469026): free under the Dubai Open Data
  Licence, but DM asks for a GeoDubai request before use on a website. Once approved: swap the units, add sectors.
- **Population by community** (Dubai Statistics Center): needs DM's community numbers. Then a population lens.

## Not available
- **Live bus and Metro times**: RTA publishes no open GTFS-realtime feed.
- **Representatives, where to vote**: no elected municipal offices; the Federal National Council is chosen by an
  appointed electoral college. Not applicable.
- **Parking zones** (Parkin), **bike share docks feed** (Careem BIKE): no open data found.
- **Schools from KHDA**: its terms rule out use for profit; schools come from OpenStreetMap, unranked.
- **Sharjah open data**: none public (Mada is government-only). OpenStreetMap only.

## Later
- Makani numbers on the card (Data Dubai 462928, 242 MB of entrances): "nearest Makani entrance" offline.
- DLD rents, aggregated by area (Data Dubai, not yet found by search).
- Recurring refreshes of Data Dubai files need a UAE address: by hand from the Mac each quarter.
