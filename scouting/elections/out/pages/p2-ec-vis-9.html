Vote in person

You can vote in person at your Elections Canada office, any time before 6:00 p.m. (local time) on December 1.

Go to your local Elections Canada office. 

Vote by mail

You can vote by mail if you apply by December 1, 6 p.m. (Eastern time).

Fill out the form here.

Vote at advance polls

Click on 'Where do I vote?' to get advanced polling dates and hours. Additional advance polling locations may be available in some remote communities, and voting hours may vary.

The address of your assigned polling place will be on the voter information card that you will receive in the mail.

You can also find the address here after November 10.

Vote on election day

You can vote at your assigned polling place on Monday, December 7, 2026.

The address of your assigned polling place will be on the voter information card that you will receive in the mail.

You can also find the address here after November 10.
