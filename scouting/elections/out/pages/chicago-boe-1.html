Election Day Judges

Become a Judge of Election

Help Chicago voters when they go to the polls next for the General Election on November 3, 2026

As an Election Judge, you get paid to manage the precinct polling place and assist voters. Election Judges share these responsibilities:

  • Arrive at 5:00 a.m. at the assigned polling place location before the polls open on Election Day to set up equipment and all necessary materials
  • Open the polls on time at 6:00 a.m. on Election Day
  • Issue the correct ballots to each voter and help voters with registration and other questions, from 6:00 a.m. through 7:00 p.m.
  • Complete all reports after the polls close, which are usually done by 8:30 p.m., sometimes later

To apply online as a Judge of Election, go to: pollworker.chicagoelections.gov 

Click here to download a paper version of the application to serve as a Judge of Election - May 2026 to April 2028

Requirements to Serve as a Judge of Election

Judges of Election must meet all of the requirements listed in the application, including:

  • A U.S. citizen
  • A registered voter in Cook County (if 18 years or older)
  • Able to speak, read and write English

Please refer to the application for the full list of requirements and qualifications.

High School and College Student 
Judges of Election

The deadline for High School Student Election Judges to apply has passed.

Requirements to Serve as a High School Student Election Judge

High School Student Election Judges must meet all of the requirements listed in the application, including: 

  • A U.S. citizen
  • A Cook County resident
  • A high school junior or senior
  • Able to read, write, and speak English
  • Successful completion of the training program
  • Able to work on Election Day from 5:00 a.m. until all duties are completed after the polls close
  • Have written approval from the High School Student Election Judge's parent or legal guardian
  • Have written approval from the High School Student Election Judge’s school principal
  • Have at least a 3.0 GPA on a 4.0 GPA scale
  • Meet any other qualifications set by law

Click here for High School Student Election Judge Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Requirements to Serve as a College Student Election Judge

To apply online as a College Student Election Judge, go to: pollworker.chicagoelections.gov

College Student Election Judges must meet all of the requirements listed in the application, including: 

  • A U.S. citizen
  • Attend a college or university
  • Able to read, write, and speak English
  • Successful completion of the training program
  • Able to work on Election Day from 5:00 a.m. until all duties are completed after the polls close
  • Have at least a 3.0 GPA on a 4.0 GPA scale
  • Meet any other qualifications set by law
     

Payment for Judges of Election

Election Judges can earn anywhere from $170 to $230. Payment is based on successful completion of the training program and service on Election Day. Election Judges must submit a W-4 federal tax form in order to receive compensation.

  • Instructions on How to Submit Your W-4 federal tax form
  • W-4 federal tax form

Extra compensation also is paid to Election Judges who:

  • Pick up the Election Judge key envelope the week before Election Day
  • Allow the use of the Election Judge's cell phone (by all Election Judges within the polling place) on Election Day
  • Return the election materials to a receiving station on Election Night (to be determined by all Election Judges on Election Day)
