Arriving

Stadium-Chinatown (Expo Line) is on Dunsmuir Street, a block east; Vancouver City Centre (Canada Line) is five blocks west on West Georgia.

The EasyPark parkade under the theatre is entered from Cambie Street between West Georgia and Dunsmuir (6'4" clearance) and fills early; the library's underground lot off Hamilton and a surface lot off Cambie near Dunsmuir are close. Taxis and drop-offs use the driveway on Hamilton between West Georgia and Dunsmuir.

Large bags go to the paid coat check, and wheeled items other than wheelchairs stay in the lobby.

Drive times are OSRM’s on empty roads, the same best-case figure as the map’s suburban lens; game nights run far slower. The taxi figure is the Metro Vancouver taxi meter rate ($3.75 plus $2.18/km, as of 2026-11) on that distance, the only published benchmark; rideshare prices aren’t published and surge after events. One fare covers 90 minutes on bus, SkyTrain and SeaBus. With a Compass Card, SkyTrain and SeaBus trips across zones cost $4.20 (2 zones) or $5.40 (3 zones) on weekdays before 6:30 pm; after 6:30 pm on weekdays, on weekends and holidays, and on every bus, any trip is 1 zone ($2.85). A contactless card or phone pays the cash fare ($3.50, $5.10 or $6.70), and a DayPass for all zones is $12.55. Trips that start at YVR-Airport, Sea Island Centre or Templeton add the $6.50 YVR AddFare. Taxis from YVR add $4 when the trip ends outside the Vancouver and Richmond flat-fare zones; tip is extra.
