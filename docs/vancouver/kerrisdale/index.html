About West Side

West of Ontario Street, from Kitsilano and Point Grey down to Marpole.

What stands out

  • Among the leafiest in Vancouver (63% tree canopy)
  • Among the most detached houses in Vancouver (45% of homes)
  • Among the longest transit trips to Waterfront in Vancouver (about 46 min)
