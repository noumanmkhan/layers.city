The same 158 neighbourhoods as the map, counted in less serious ways. Each map is a head-to-head or a signature: what a neighbourhood has more of than the rest of the city.

Shops and restaurants © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). 311 requests, parking tickets, street names, rec facilities and EV chargers: City of Toronto Open Data, NRCan/AFDC. Counts are only as complete as their sources. Refreshed every three months.
