Arriving

Inside Exhibition Place, on the west side of the grounds. Exhibition GO (Lakeshore West) and the 509 Harbourfront and 511 Bathurst streetcars at Exhibition Loop are the transit way in; the walk from the loop crosses the grounds.

Exhibition Place runs its own paid lots across the grounds; they are not Green P. On big match days the lots fill and Lake Shore Boulevard slows well before kickoff.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check bmofield.com before you go.
