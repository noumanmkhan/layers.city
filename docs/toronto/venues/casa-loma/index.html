Arriving

The castle on the Davenport hill, a steep ten-minute walk up from Dupont station on Line 1 (the Baldwin Steps). Its events are seasonal productions on the grounds, the Halloween run above all; parking is a small paid lot on Austin Terrace and street parking is restricted, so transit or a drop-off is the usual plan.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check casaloma.ca before you go.
