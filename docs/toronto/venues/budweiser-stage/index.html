Arriving

Open-air amphitheatre at Ontario Place, across Lake Shore Boulevard from Exhibition Place. Exhibition GO and the Exhibition streetcar loop are the transit way in; from the loop it is a walk south across Lake Shore on the pedestrian bridge.

Parking is the Ontario Place and Exhibition Place lots, which sell out for big shows; the Lake Shore and Gardiner approaches jam before doors.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check budweiserstage.com before you go.
