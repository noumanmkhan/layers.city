Arriving

Open-air, built on the former Downsview Airport lands and opened in June 2025. The pedestrian entrance is at 105 Carl Hall Road; the venue names its gates by number (Gate 1 and Gate 3 are the main ones) and any ticket works at any gate.

There is next to no parking at the site and the venue says so. Plan on the subway: Sheppard West and Downsview Park on Line 1 are the stations the venue points to, and Downsview Park is also a GO station on the Barrie line.

Bags: small clutches, belt bags and clear bags only; no backpacks. Gate and plaza hours come by email per show.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check rogers-stadium.com/know-before-you-go before you go.
