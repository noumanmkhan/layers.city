Arriving

Mississauga's arena (Paramount Fine Foods Centre until 2026, the Hershey Centre before that), just south of Square One off Hurontario. Cooksville GO (Milton line) and MiWay buses on Hurontario serve it; the Hazel McCallion LRT on Hurontario is the long-term link. It has its own lots, which is part of why GTA-west fans choose it.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check paramountfinefoodscentre.com before you go.
