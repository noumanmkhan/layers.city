Arriving

Beside the CN Tower. From Union Station the covered SkyWalk runs west from the station's west end to the stadium, about a ten-minute walk, so most people arrive on foot from Union rather than driving in.

Gates are numbered around the building; the Bremner Boulevard side faces the CN Tower and Union, the Blue Jays Way side faces the hotel. Your ticket names the gate nearest your section.

Game-day parking downtown fills early and the Gardiner's Spadina, York and Jarvis ramps are the usual approach; Green P garages within a ten-minute walk are listed below.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check mlb.com/bluejays/ballpark/transportation before you go.
