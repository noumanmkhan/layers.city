Arriving

Attached to Union Station: the Galleria connects the arena's Bay Street side to the station under cover, so GO, UP Express and Line 1 all land at the door. Entrances face Bay Street, Maple Leaf Square to the north and Lake Shore Boulevard to the south.

The arena's own garage is for suite holders and tenants (a flat $50 for the public when open, 2 m height limit). The public garage under Maple Leaf Square (15 York Street, entrances on York and Bremner) and the Green P garages listed below are the usual choices.
