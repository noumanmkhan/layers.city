Arriving

The 7 train and LIRR Port Washington Branch trains stop at Mets-Willets Point, and from there a boardwalk leads to the Main Entry.

Rideshare drop-off is in the New York Hall of Science lot, with a path to the Champion's Entry.

Both entrances screen bags, and large bags are not allowed.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2026-03-04/how_to_get_to_the_us_open.html before you go.
