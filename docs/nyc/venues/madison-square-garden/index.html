Arriving

Above Penn Station, between Seventh and Eighth Avenues from 31st to 33rd Street, so the LIRR, NJ Transit and the 1, 2, 3, A, C and E trains stop underneath.

The Chase entrances are on Eighth Avenue, and there is a Seventh Avenue entrance too.

The Garden has no parking of its own; drivers use nearby commercial garages.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check msg.com/madison-square-garden before you go.
