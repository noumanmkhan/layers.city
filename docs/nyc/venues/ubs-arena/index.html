Arriving

LIRR trains from Grand Central Madison and Penn Station reach Elmont-UBS Arena station in about 30 minutes, then it is a 10 to 15 minute walk or a free shuttle to the arena.

Parking is paid, in lots reached only from the Cross Island Parkway (exits 26A, 26B and 26D), and drivers must leave the way they came.

Bags up to 12 x 12 x 6 inches are allowed; backpacks of any size are not, and there is a bag check in the Acrisure Great Hall.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check ubsarena.com/getting-here before you go.

From across the region

By transit, from anywhere in the region: LIRR to Elmont-UBS Arena (about 30 min from Penn Station or Grand Central Madison; from elsewhere on Long Island, change at Jamaica), then a 10 to 15 min walk or the free shuttle.
