Arriving

On Sixth Avenue between 50th and 51st Streets in Rockefeller Center, with the box office under the marquee at 50th Street.

Bags must fit under the seat, bags larger than 22 x 14 x 9 inches are not allowed, and there is no bag or coat check.

There is no venue parking; drivers use nearby commercial garages.
