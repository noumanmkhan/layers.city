Getting there

Meadowlands · about 3 min on foot · NJ Transit

Arriving

On big event days NJ Transit runs Meadowlands Rail trains from Secaucus Junction to the stadium's own Meadowlands station, and riders show a valid train ticket at Secaucus to get a wristband.

Coach USA's 351 Meadowlands Express runs from the Port Authority Bus Terminal to Lot K.

The entrances are the AMEX, MetLife and Verizon gates; parking is paid, and only clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches (or a small clutch) are allowed, one per person.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check metlifestadium.com before you go.

From across the region

By transit, from anywhere in the region: On event days, NJ Transit to Secaucus Junction (from Penn Station or Hoboken), then the Meadowlands train to the stadium; or Coach USA’s 351 from the Port Authority Bus Terminal.
