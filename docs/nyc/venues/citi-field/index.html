Arriving

The 7 train and the LIRR Port Washington Branch both stop at Mets-Willets Point beside the ballpark.

The main entry is the Jackie Robinson Rotunda, with Left Field, Right Field and Bullpen gates as well (the Bullpen gate is on Seaver Way).

Backpacks are not allowed, other bags must be no larger than 16 x 16 x 8 inches and there is no bag check; paid game-day parking is in Lot F.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check mlb.com/mets/ballpark/information/guide before you go.
