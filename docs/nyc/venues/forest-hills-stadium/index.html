Arriving

The entrance is on Burns Street near 69th Avenue, a few blocks from the Forest Hills-71 Av subway station and next to the LIRR's Forest Hills station.

There is no parking at the venue or on the residential streets around it, and taxis and rideshares use 71st/Continental Avenue at Queens Boulevard.

Bags up to 16 x 16 x 8 inches are allowed, and larger bags must be checked for a fee.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check foresthillsstadium.com/get-here before you go.
