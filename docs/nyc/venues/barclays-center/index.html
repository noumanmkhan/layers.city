Arriving

Above the Atlantic Av-Barclays Ctr subway station (2, 3, 4, 5, B, D, N, Q, R) and across from the LIRR's Atlantic Terminal.

The main Atrium entrance is at the corner of Flatbush and Atlantic Avenues, and there are also Flatbush, Atlantic and Dean Street entrances.

Backpacks and bags larger than 10 x 6 x 2 inches are not allowed, and the venue says parking nearby is very limited.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check barclayscenter.com/a-z-guide before you go.
