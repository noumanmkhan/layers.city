Arriving

At Flatbush Avenue Extension and DeKalb Avenue, across the street from DeKalb Av (B, Q, R), with Nevins St (2, 3, 4, 5) about 0.2 miles away.

Allowed bags are clear ones up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches or a small bag up to 6 x 9 inches, and larger bags must be checked for a fee.

Tickets are mobile only, and parking is in nearby garages.
