About Queens

The largest borough by area, east of Brooklyn on Long Island, home to both of the city’s airports.

What stands out

  • Among the most detached houses in New York City (42% of homes)
  • Among the highest shares of homeowners in Queens (31% rent)
  • Among the oldest housing in Queens (39% built 1939 or earlier)
