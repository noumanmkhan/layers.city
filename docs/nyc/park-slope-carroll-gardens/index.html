About Brooklyn

The most populous borough, at the western end of Long Island, across the East River from Lower Manhattan.

Names you’ll hear here

  • Cobble Hill
  • Gowanus
  • Red Hook

What stands out

  • Oldest housing in New York City (66% built 1939 or earlier)
  • Among the highest home values in New York City (median $1.7M)
  • Among the highest rents in Brooklyn (median $2,750/mo)
