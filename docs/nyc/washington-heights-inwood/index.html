About Manhattan

The island between the Hudson and East rivers: downtown, Midtown, the Upper East and West Sides, Harlem and Upper Manhattan.

What stands out

  • Among the most homes in large apartment buildings in New York City (87% of homes)
  • Among the most affordable homes in Manhattan (median $590K)
  • Leafiest in Manhattan (35% tree canopy)
