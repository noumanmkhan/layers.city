Attributions

Every map here is built from other people’s open data and open-source software. This page lists each source, what it’s used for, and the terms it’s used under.

Toronto Chicago Both cities Software and fonts

In short

  • The maps are drawn from public data published by governments, transit agencies and open projects such as OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia. No map tiles are borrowed from other sites.
  • Each data source is used under its own licence or terms of use, linked below. Where a licence asks for credit, the credit is also shown on the map itself.
  • The data is reshaped for the maps (simplified, merged, summarised). Any mistakes in that are mine, not the publishers’, and no publisher endorses these maps.
  • The City Layers iOS app reads the same published files and credits the same sources.

Toronto Layers

City of Toronto

  • City of Toronto Open Data

    Neighbourhoods, wards, former municipalities, land and water, street centrelines and expressways, Business Improvement Areas, Heritage Conservation Districts, Parks and Recreation facilities, Toronto Public Library branches, Neighbourhood Profiles (2021 Census), road restrictions for transit construction, and the TTC’s schedules (GTFS).

    Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Toronto
  • Statistics Canada, 2021 Census of Population

    Housing, commuting, home types, year built, language, ages and recent immigration, through the City’s Neighbourhood Profiles.

    Statistics Canada Open Licence
  • City of Toronto, City Clerk’s Office

    The 2026 school board ward reference chart (which City wards make up each trustee ward), and the 2026 election candidates and unofficial results.

    Public election information, toronto.ca

Transit

  • Toronto Transit Commission (TTC)

    Streetcar and bus routes, stops and frequencies, subway and LRT lines, and transit times to Union Station. Published through City of Toronto Open Data.

    Open Government Licence – Toronto
  • Metrolinx (GO Transit and UP Express)

    GO train lines and stations, GO bus routes and stops, and GO and UP Express trips in the transit-time lens.

    Open Government Licence – Ontario – Metrolinx

Representatives and ridings

  • Elections Canada, via Open North’s Represent

    Federal electoral district boundaries (2023 Representation Order).

    Open Government Licence – Canada
  • Elections Ontario, via Open North’s Represent

    Provincial riding names only. The riding shapes on the map are built from the City’s wards, which share the same lines.

    Elections Ontario data licence
  • House of Commons, Legislative Assembly of Ontario and City of Toronto

    Names of the current MP, MPP and city councillor for each riding and ward, from each body’s public member list.

    Public information: ourcommons.ca, ola.org, toronto.ca

Chicago Layers

City of Chicago and Chicago Public Schools

  • City of Chicago Data Portal

    Community areas, neighbourhoods, wards and ward offices, the city boundary, Special Service Areas, Landmark Districts, the ‘L’ lines and stops, library branches, DFSS community service centres, CDOT permits for transit construction, and Chicago Public Schools attendance boundaries and school profiles.

    City of Chicago Data Portal Terms of Use
  • Chicago Park District

    Names and addresses of Park District fieldhouses, for the nearest community centre on the card.

    Public facility list, chicagoparkdistrict.com

U.S. government

  • U.S. Census Bureau

    American Community Survey 2020–2024 tables (housing, commuting, home types, year built, language, ages, period of entry); TIGER boundaries for counties, municipalities, congressional and state legislative districts, tracts and Lake Michigan; and the Census Geocoder for placing addresses.

    Public domain (U.S. government work); Census Bureau terms
  • U.S. Department of Education, NCES (IPEDS)

    Which universities and colleges appear, and their campuses.

    Public domain (U.S. government work)
  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services

    Which hospitals have an emergency department, and their ownership.

    Public domain (U.S. government work), data.cms.gov
  • Clerk of the U.S. House of Representatives

    The current U.S. representative for each congressional district.

    Public domain (U.S. government work)

Transit

  • Chicago Transit Authority (CTA)

    Bus routes, stops and frequencies, and CTA trips in the transit-time lens. Data provided by Chicago Transit Authority.

    CTA Developer License Agreement & Terms of Use
  • Metra

    Metra lines, stations and trips.

    Metra developer license agreement
  • Northern Indiana Commuter Transportation District (South Shore Line)

    South Shore Line trips in the transit-time lens.

    Published GTFS feed, mysouthshoreline.com

Representatives and the school board

  • Open States (Plural)

    Current Illinois state representatives and senators.

    Public domain dedication
  • Illinois Senate Redistricting Committee

    Chicago Board of Education districts enacted by Public Act 103-0584.

    Public record, ilsenateredistricting.com
  • Chicago Board of Election Commissioners

    Board of Education candidates for November 3, 2026, and the results once posted.

    Public election information, chicagoelections.gov

Used for both cities

  • OpenStreetMap contributors

    Neighbouring municipalities and the lakeshore around Toronto, highways, Chicago’s streets and expressways, named places for search, universities, colleges and hospitals, parks and playgrounds, transit lines under construction, and the walking network for transit times. Some come through extracts by Geofabrik, BBBike and the osm-boundaries project.

    © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database Licence (ODbL)
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation)

    Address search on the website, and placing a few landmarks and towers.

    Nominatim Usage Policy
  • OSRM (Project OSRM)

    Free-flow drive times to downtown in the suburban lens, on OpenStreetMap roads.

    OSRM demo server usage policy
  • Wikipedia contributors

    Facts about skyscrapers: height, floors, year, use and location, from each city’s “List of tallest buildings” and the linked articles. The card’s short descriptions are written for this site.

    CC BY-SA 4.0; Wikidata ids CC0
  • ESA WorldCover

    Tree canopy: © ESA WorldCover project 2021 / Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021) processed by ESA WorldCover consortium.

    CC BY 4.0, esa-worldcover.org
  • Natural Earth

    The Great Lakes away from each city’s shore.

    Public domain
  • toronto-bus-map (Miqell24)

    Toronto’s subway, LRT and GO train lines and stations, extracted from that project’s files, which are built from the TTC and Metrolinx schedules credited above.

    Underlying data: TTC and Metrolinx licences above

Software and fonts

  • Leaflet

    Draws the interactive map.

    BSD 2-Clause
  • Leaflet.PolylineOffset

    Draws transit lines that share a street side by side.

    MIT
  • html-to-image

    Saves the map as a picture.

    MIT
  • mapshaper, Shapely, r5py and R5

    Build tools that simplify shapes and compute transit times. They run when the maps are built, not in your browser.

    Each under its own open-source licence
  • Inter, Archivo and IBM Plex

    Typefaces for the maps (Inter, from Google Fonts) and for these pages (Archivo, IBM Plex Sans and Mono).

    SIL Open Font License 1.1
