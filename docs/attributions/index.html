Attributions

Every map here is built from other people’s open data and open-source software. This page lists each source, what it’s used for, and the terms it’s used under.

Toronto New York City Chicago Vancouver San Francisco Dallas Every city Software and fonts

In short

  • The maps are drawn from public data published by governments, transit agencies and open projects such as OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia. No map tiles are borrowed from other sites.
  • Each data source is used under its own licence or terms of use, linked below. Where a licence asks for credit, the credit is also shown on the map itself.
  • The data is reshaped for the maps (simplified, merged, summarised). Any mistakes in that are mine, not the publishers’, and no publisher endorses these maps.
  • The City Layers iOS app reads the same published files and credits the same sources.

Toronto Layers

City of Toronto

  • City of Toronto Open Data

    Neighbourhoods, wards, former municipalities, land and water, street centrelines and expressways, Business Improvement Areas, Heritage Conservation Districts, the Cycling Network (bike lanes), the Heritage Register (individually designated heritage properties), development applications, building permits and address points (for “What’s changing”), Parks and Recreation facilities, the recreation facilities list (Registered Programs and Drop In Courses Offering, for Sports & rec), Green Spaces (park outlines), Toronto Public Library branches, school locations (for the French-language and French immersion schools), Green P car parks (the City’s 2019 list, its latest), on-street permit parking areas, City-operated EV chargers, the solid waste pickup calendar (for the card’s collection day), Neighbourhood Profiles (2021 Census), road restrictions for transit construction, the TTC’s schedules (GTFS), and, for the Fun maps page, 311 Service Requests (2025), Parking Tickets (2025, placed with the address points) and the full street centreline (for street-name endings).

    Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Toronto
  • Statistics Canada, 2021 Census of Population

    Housing, commuting, home types, year built, language, ages and recent immigration, through the City’s Neighbourhood Profiles.

    Statistics Canada Open Licence
  • City of Toronto, Solid Waste Management Services

    The residential waste collection areas (from the City’s map server, gis.toronto.ca), matched to the pickup calendar for the card’s collection day.

    Published on the City’s map server; no licence is stated on the layer
  • Bike Share Toronto (Toronto Parking Authority)

    Bike Share station locations, docks, e-bike charging and payment methods, from the system’s public GBFS feed, linked from the City’s open data portal.

    Published as an open feed; no licence is stated
  • Alternative Fuels Data Center (U.S. Department of Energy) and Natural Resources Canada

    Public EV charging stations: where they are, their Level 2 and DC fast ports, network, hours and listed prices, from the Alternative Fuels Data Center’s station API, which also holds the Canadian listings behind Natural Resources Canada’s Electric Charging and Alternative Fuelling Stations Locator.

    Public data from the AFDC station API, used under its terms of use (afdc.energy.gov)
  • OpenStreetMap contributors

    Shops, cafés and restaurants counted on the Fun maps page (coffee chains, liquor and cannabis stores, gas stations, laundromats, bookstores and the like), from the Overpass API.

    © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
  • Canada Running Series

    The TCS Toronto Waterfront Marathon course, from the official Garmin course the organizer links from the race’s Event Info page, drawn with kilometre marks, start and finish during race weekend.

    Published by the organizer for runners; no licence is stated (torontowaterfrontmarathon.com)
  • City of Toronto, City Clerk’s Office

    The 2026 school board ward reference chart (which City wards make up each trustee ward), the 2026 election candidates and unofficial results, and the 2026 voting subdivisions and election-day voting places (with building names and entrances from the City’s list of voting places) for the card’s where-to-vote box.

    Public election information, toronto.ca
  • Toronto’s four school boards

    Which schools teach French: the TDSB’s 2026–27 Early and Middle French Immersion location maps, the TCDSB’s French Immersion school lists, and the school lists of the Conseil scolaire Viamonde and the Conseil scolaire catholique MonAvenir.

    Public program information from each board’s website
  • Ontario Ministry of Education

    Every publicly funded school in Toronto, with its board, level, grades, address, location and website, from “School information and student demographics” on the Ontario Data Catalogue (2024–25 school year). Only those descriptive fields are used; test results and student figures are not.

    Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Ontario

Transit

  • Toronto Transit Commission (TTC)

    Streetcar and bus routes, stops and frequencies, subway and LRT lines, and transit times to Union Station; planned subway closures in the iPhone app’s weekend summary, from the TTC’s GTFS-Realtime service alerts; live bus, streetcar and subway arrival times on the Where does this line or route go? page, from its GTFS-Realtime trip updates; subway and LRT lines and platforms on that page, from its schedules. Published through City of Toronto Open Data.

    Open Government Licence – Toronto
  • Metrolinx (GO Transit and UP Express)

    GO train lines and stations, GO bus routes and stops, and GO and UP Express trips in the transit-time lens.

    Open Government Licence – Ontario – Metrolinx

Representatives and ridings

    Open Government Licence – Canada
  • Elections Ontario, via Open North’s Represent

    Provincial riding names only. The riding shapes on the map are built from the City’s wards, which share the same lines.

    Elections Ontario data licence
  • House of Commons, Legislative Assembly of Ontario and City of Toronto

    Names of the current MP, MPP and city councillor for each riding and ward, from each body’s public member list.

    Public information: ourcommons.ca, ola.org, toronto.ca
  • Halal Monitoring Authority (HMA)

    The halal grocers and butchers layer: HMA-certified independent stores and their addresses, from HMA’s public list of certified stores.

    Public information: hmacanada.org
  • COR (Kashruth Council of Canada)

    The kosher grocers, butchers and bakeries layer: COR-certified independent shops and their addresses, from COR’s public directory.

    Public information: cor.ca
  • Ticketmaster

    The Venues pages’ event listings: event name, date, time and kind for the coming two weeks at each listed venue, refreshed nightly from the Ticketmaster Discovery API. Each event links to its own page on Ticketmaster; nothing else from the feed is kept, and Layers.City sells nothing and carries no affiliate links. Event content belongs to the organizers and Ticketmaster and is removed on request.

    Used under the Ticketmaster API Terms of Use: developer.ticketmaster.com
