Arriving

Inside Fair Park, a short walk from Fair Park Station on DART's Green Line.

Drivers enter at Gate 5 (Robert B. Cullum Boulevard and Grand Avenue); Gate 4 at First and Parry Avenues is closed. General parking is included with Broadway Dallas series tickets and paid on arrival for other events; valet is at the circular drive and can't be booked ahead.

Accessible spaces next to the hall are first come: enter at Gate 5, turn left on Second Avenue, then right into the upper lot.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check broadwaydallas.org/?p=17283 before you go.

Drive times are OSRM’s on empty roads, the same best-case figure as the map’s suburban lens; game nights run far slower. The taxi figure is the City of Dallas taxi meter rate ($3.00 plus $2.80/mi, as of 2026-11) on that distance, the only published benchmark; rideshare prices aren’t published and surge after events. A DART 3-Hour Pass covers buses, light rail, the Silver Line and the TRE as far west as CentrePort; a local Day Pass is $6, and riders who tap a GoPass Tap card or a contactless card never pay more than $6 in a day. The TRE west of CentrePort (Fort Worth and the rest of Tarrant County) and DCTA's A-train need a Regional Day Pass ($9). Dallas taxis add $2 for each extra passenger and a fuel surcharge the City sets each quarter; trips between downtown and DFW Airport ($55) or Love Field ($26) are flat fares; tolls and tip are extra.
