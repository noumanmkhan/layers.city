Arriving

In the Arts District. Lexus Red Parking is under the opera house, entered from the north end of Jack Evans Street; it can be bought ahead until two hours before curtain or paid at the gate (cash or card), and attendants are there from 2.5 hours before.

Drop-offs are on Flora Street. The lobby usually opens 90 minutes before curtain and the hall about 30 minutes before.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check attpac.org/visit/parking before you go.

The taxi figure is the City of Dallas taxi meter rate ($3.00 plus $2.80/mi, as of 2026-11) on that distance, the only published benchmark; rideshare prices aren’t published and surge after events. A DART 3-Hour Pass covers buses, light rail, the Silver Line and the TRE as far west as CentrePort; a local Day Pass is $6, and riders who tap a GoPass Tap card or a contactless card never pay more than $6 in a day. The TRE west of CentrePort (Fort Worth and the rest of Tarrant County) and DCTA's A-train need a Regional Day Pass ($9). Dallas taxis add $2 for each extra passenger and a fuel surcharge the City sets each quarter; trips between downtown and DFW Airport ($55) or Love Field ($26) are flat fares; tolls and tip are extra.
