Arriving

The West Entry, on Victory Avenue between Nowitzki Way and All Star Way, faces Victory Station: DART's Green and Orange Lines stop there every day, and TRE trains use platforms 3 and 4 (no Sunday TRE service unless announced). From the Red and Blue Lines, change at West End.

Only wallets, wristlets and clutches up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches are allowed at every entrance; purses up to 14 x 6 x 14 inches only at the X-ray entrances, and no backpacks or large totes. The arena is cashless.

Parking is in the Victory Park garages (Fifth Third, Commons, Lexus and D) and lots, most within two blocks; most open two hours before the event and the Lexus Garage at noon, with no in-and-out. Rideshares drop off and pick up on the north side of the arena, on All-Star Way and Nowitzki Way.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check americanairlinescenter.com/plan-your-visit/arena-faq before you go.

Drive times are OSRM’s on empty roads, the same best-case figure as the map’s suburban lens; game nights run far slower. The taxi figure is the City of Dallas taxi meter rate ($3.00 plus $2.80/mi, as of 2026-11) on that distance, the only published benchmark; rideshare prices aren’t published and surge after events. A DART 3-Hour Pass covers buses, light rail, the Silver Line and the TRE as far west as CentrePort; a local Day Pass is $6, and riders who tap a GoPass Tap card or a contactless card never pay more than $6 in a day. The TRE west of CentrePort (Fort Worth and the rest of Tarrant County) and DCTA's A-train need a Regional Day Pass ($9). Dallas taxis add $2 for each extra passenger and a fuel surcharge the City sets each quarter; trips between downtown and DFW Airport ($55) or Love Field ($26) are flat fares; tolls and tip are extra.
