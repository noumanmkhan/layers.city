Arriving

The Red, Orange and Green Lines stop at Roosevelt; from there it is a walk east along Roosevelt Road through the Museum Campus, or the #146 bus, which serves the stadium all year. On Bears game days the #128 Soldier Field Express runs non-stop from Ogilvie and Union Station, and Pace runs Soldier Field Express buses from suburban park-and-ride lots.

Will Call is across from Gate 10 on the southeast corner. Only clear bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches (or a one-gallon clear freezer bag) are allowed, plus a small non-clear clutch up to 4.5 x 6.5 inches.

The lots around the stadium are paid and need a prepaid pass on Bears game days (sold at SoldierFieldParking.com); free shuttles run from the Millennium Garages and McCormick Place Lot B. Rideshares and taxis drop off and pick up at Balbo Drive and Columbus Drive.

Written from the venue’s own visitor pages; gates and policies change, so check soldierfield.com/stadium-info/a-z-guide before you go.

Drive times are OSRM’s on empty roads, the same best-case figure as the map’s suburban lens; game nights run far slower. The taxi figure is the City of Chicago taxi meter rate ($3.25 plus $2.79/mi, as of 2026-11) on that distance, the only published benchmark; rideshare prices aren’t published and surge after events. CTA transfers are free for two more rides within two hours; a 1-day CTA/Pace pass is $5 and a 7-day $20. Metra one-way fares run from $3.75 (within Chicago, or between suburban zones) to $6.75 (zone 4 to downtown), and a weekend pass is $10; Metra and CTA fares are paid separately. Chicago taxis add $1 for a first extra passenger and $0.50 for each one after, $2.50 from 3:30 to 7 pm and $1 from 8 pm to 6 am, a $4 departure tax from the airports and a temporary fuel surcharge of $1 to $3; tolls and tip are extra.
