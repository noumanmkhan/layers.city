Drive times are OSRM’s on empty roads, the same best-case figure as the map’s suburban lens; game nights run far slower. The taxi figure is the City of Chicago taxi meter rate ($3.25 plus $2.79/mi, as of 2026-11) on that distance, the only published benchmark; rideshare prices aren’t published and surge after events. CTA transfers are free for two more rides within two hours; a 1-day CTA/Pace pass is $5 and a 7-day $20. Metra one-way fares run from $3.75 (within Chicago, or between suburban zones) to $6.75 (zone 4 to downtown), and a weekend pass is $10; Metra and CTA fares are paid separately. Chicago taxis add $1 for a first extra passenger and $0.50 for each one after, $2.50 from 3:30 to 7 pm and $1 from 8 pm to 6 am, a $4 departure tax from the airports and a temporary fuel surcharge of $1 to $3; tolls and tip are extra.
