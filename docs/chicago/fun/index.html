Fun maps

The same 77 community areas as the map, counted in less serious ways. Each map is a head-to-head or a signature: what a community area has more of than the rest of the city.

Shops and restaurants © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). 311 requests: City of Chicago Data Portal. Courts: OpenStreetMap, as on the map's Sports & rec layer. EV chargers: U.S. DOE Alternative Fuels Data Center. Counts are only as complete as their sources. Refreshed every three months.
